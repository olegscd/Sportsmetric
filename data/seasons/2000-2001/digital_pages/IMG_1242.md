### LEGEND:
* **GP** — GAMES PLAYED
* **W** — WINS
* **L** — LOSSES
* **PF** — POINTS FOR
* **PA** — POINTS AGAINTS
* **REB** — REBOUNDS
  * **O** — OFFENSIVE
  * **D** — DEFENSIVE
* **A** — ASSIST
* **ST/INT** — STEALS/INTERCEPTION
* **BS** — BLOCKS SHOTS
* **E/TO** — ERRORS/TURNOVERS
* **FW/F** — FOULS/FRE THROW
* **FW/OF** — FOULS WITH FRE THROW *

*ARRANGED ACCORDING TO ORDER OF HIGHER QUOTIENT
