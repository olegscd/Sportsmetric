### Statistical Points Computation:

$$ \frac{\text{[(Total Pts + Total Rebs + Total Asts + Total Stls + Total Blks) * 2] – Total TOs – Penalty Pts + Bonus Pts}}}{\text{Number of Games Played}} = \text{Statistical Points} $$

* **Penalty Points** = 5 for every Technical & Unsportsmanlike Fouls, 10 for Ejection from the Game
* **Bonus Points** = 15 Points for every game won in which a player played
