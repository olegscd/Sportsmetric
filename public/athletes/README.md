# Bundled athlete portraits

Square, resized copies of photographs published on Wikimedia Commons. They are
shown on international athlete cards and profiles with the credit and license
printed alongside the portrait.

The images were cropped to focus on each athlete and resized to 480×480 for use
as avatars. Cropped versions of the CC BY-SA 4.0 photograph remain available
under CC BY-SA 4.0; the CC BY 4.0 photograph remains available under CC BY 4.0.
